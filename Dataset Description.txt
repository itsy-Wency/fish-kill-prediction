Sensor-Based Aquaponics Fish Pond Dataset (Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ogbuokiriblessing/sensor-based-aquaponics-fish-pond-datasets?)
Model Trained by: Wency N. Jorda

This dataset contains time-series water quality measurements collected from an 
IoT-enabled aquaponics fish pond monitoring system. The data were gathered using 
multiple environmental sensors deployed across several aquaponics ponds to continuously 
monitor critical water parameters affecting fish health and aquatic ecosystem stability.


Data Collection
The dataset was generated using an Internet of Things (IoT) monitoring system equipped 
with water quality sensors connected to a microcontroller and cloud logging platform. 
Sensor readings were recorded at regular intervals to produce a high-resolution time-series 
dataset.

Measurements were taken from multiple aquaponics ponds, 
enabling analysis of water quality variations across different pond environments.



Parameters Included
The dataset contains several important water quality indicators commonly 
used in aquaculture monitoring:

Parameter	           Description
1.Timestamp	           Date and time when the sensor reading was recorded
2.Temperature	           Water temperature (°C) affecting fish metabolism and oxygen solubility
3.pH	                   Acidity or alkalinity of the water
4.Dissolved Oxygen (DO)	   Amount of oxygen dissolved in water, critical for fish respiration
5.Turbidity	           Cloudiness of the water caused by suspended particles
6.Ammonia 	           Concentration of ammonia, which can become toxic to 
                           fish at high levels
7.Nitrate	                   Nutrient concentration indicating nitrogen cycle activity
8.Fish Length	           Recorded length of fish in the monitored pond
9.Fish Weight	           Recorded weight of fish in the monitored pond

Dataset Characteristics
Type: Multivariate time-series dataset
Domain: Aquaculture / Aquaponics monitoring
Data source: IoT water quality sensor network
Sampling frequency: High-frequency sensor readings
